Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you give us directly, along with a small amount of technical data needed to keep your account secure. This includes:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and password
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage and log data, such as login timestamps and browser type

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect solely to operate and improve Spendly for you. This includes:

  • Displaying your expense history, categories, and spending summaries
  • Authenticating your account and keeping it secure
  • Diagnosing bugs and improving the reliability of the Service

We do not use your financial data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on a small number of third-party services to operate reliably, such as hosting providers and font delivery services. These providers process data only as needed to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, or if you would like to request that your data be corrected or deleted, please reach out to us through your account settings or the contact details provided when you signed up.
